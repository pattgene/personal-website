Patt PHURTIVILAI

Hello! I’m Patt Phurtivilai, a research postgraduate in Artificial Intelligence at the University of Hong Kong. I conduct 3D vision research in CGVU Lab under the supervision of Prof. Taku Komura.

I am passionate about building AI that understands and interacts with the real 3D world. My research focuses on computer vision and graphics, including 3D tracking, reconstruction, and neural rendering.

My current work explores self-supervised multi-view tracking and 3D perception systems developed through collaborations with academic and industry researchers.
